About Us

A home for curious minds

Warthog Academy is a registered secondary school under the Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education, offering both boarding and day schooling for Forms 1 to 4. We combine strong academics with sport and practical skills so every learner can thrive.

With a low teacher-to-student ratio, qualified and experienced staff, and full-day classes, we create a conducive learning environment where every child is seen, supported and stretched toward their potential.
